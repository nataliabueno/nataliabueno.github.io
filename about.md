I am a political scientist interested in public policy, housing policy, elections, and comparative politics. I am an associate professor at Emory University.

I examine the causes of nonstate welfare provision and the consequences of public policies, specifically housing policy, on political behavior and elections. My teaching focuses on Latin American politics, public policy, and research methods. I use a multi-method approach with an emphasis on causal inference.

My research has been published or is forthcoming in *American Journal of Political Science*, *American Political Science Review*, *British Journal of Political Science*, *Comparative Political Studies*, *Journal of Experimental Political Science*, *Journal of Quantitative Description: Digital Media*, *Journal of Politics*, *Latin American Research Review*, *Science Advances*, and *World Politics*, among other outlets.

I received the Clarence Stone Scholar Award from APSA's Urban and Local Politics Section in 2024. My research on nonstate welfare provision has received APSA's Harold D. Lasswell Award for best dissertation in the field of public policy (2019), the James G. March Award from Yale's Political Science department (2018), and an honorable mention for the Sérgio Buarque de Holanda Award for best paper in the social sciences from the Latin American Studies Association's Brazil Section (2019). My collaborative work on public policy and housing has received the Best Paper on Public Policy Award from APSA's Public Policy Section (2021), the Best Paper Award from REPAL (Red para el Estudio de la Economía Política de América Latina, 2022), the Best Comparative Policy Paper Award from APSA's Public Policy Section (2024), and the Deil S. Wright Best Paper Award from APSA's Federalism & Intergovernmental Relations Section (2026). My collaborative research on elections and misinformation received the award for Best Research Article published in the *Journal of Experimental Political Science* in 2024 from APSA's Experimental Research Section.

I run the [Local Politics & Policy Lab](https://localpoliticslab.org/) at Emory University, alongside [Clara Bicalho](https://clarabicalho.github.io/), where we examine questions of local governments' service provision and housing with a global perspective.

I am also a senior researcher at [ConnectLab](https://eesp.fgv.br/centros-de-estudos/connectlab) at the São Paulo School of Economics (FGV EESP), where we conduct research focused on issues of public interest related to elections, information, and democracy.

My work has been supported by Meta, Stanford Internet Observatory, MIT Gov/Lab, EGAP LatAm, SciBR, CNPq, Yale's ISPS and MacMillan Center, and IPEA, among others.
